# Delivery Policy

**Last Updated:** [DATE]  
**Effective Date:** [DATE]  
**Policy Version:** [VERSION]

This Delivery Policy explains how orders placed through **[WEBSITE / BUSINESS NAME]** are delivered to customers.

The exact delivery area, charges, schedules, and operating hours are configured by the Business and may change over time.

---

## 1. Delivery Availability

Delivery is available only within the areas currently served by the Business.

**Current Delivery Area:** [DELIVERY AREA]

The Business may limit delivery availability based on:

- distance from the shop;
- delivery capacity;
- operating hours;
- product requirements;
- weather or road conditions;
- availability of delivery staff;
- other operational circumstances.

An address may be checked for delivery availability before an order is accepted.

---

## 2. Delivery Address

Customers must provide an accurate and complete delivery address when placing an order.

The delivery address may include information such as:

- house or building number;
- street or locality;
- city;
- postal code;
- relevant access instructions;
- recipient information where required.

Customers are responsible for checking the address before confirming the order.

An incorrect or incomplete address may result in delivery delays or inability to complete delivery.

---

## 3. Delivery Date and Time

Where scheduled delivery is available, customers may select an available delivery date and time during checkout.

**Available delivery schedule:** [DELIVERY DAYS / HOURS]

The selected delivery time represents the requested or scheduled delivery period.

The Business will make reasonable efforts to deliver within the selected period but cannot guarantee an exact arrival time where circumstances outside its reasonable control affect delivery.

---

## 4. Delivery Charges

Delivery charges may apply depending on the customer's location.

**Delivery Fee Structure:** [DELIVERY FEE RULE]

Delivery fees may be calculated using factors such as:

- distance from the Business;
- delivery area;
- order characteristics;
- delivery time;
- other Business-defined rules.

The applicable delivery charge should be displayed to the customer before the order is confirmed.

The Business may change its delivery charges for future orders.

---

## 5. Customer Availability

Customers should ensure that someone is reasonably available to receive the order during the selected delivery period.

For products such as cakes and sweets, prompt receipt is important because they may be perishable or sensitive to temperature and handling conditions.

If the recipient cannot be reached, the delivery person or Business staff may attempt to contact the customer using the contact information provided with the order.

---

## 6. Failed Delivery Caused by the Customer

Delivery may be considered unsuccessful where the Business is unable to complete delivery because of circumstances attributable to the customer, including:

- incorrect address;
- incomplete address;
- unavailable recipient;
- refusal to receive the order;
- inability to access the provided location;
- failure to respond to reasonable delivery communication.

The Business may attempt to arrange another delivery where reasonably possible.

Additional delivery charges may apply where permitted by the Business's current delivery rules.

**Redelivery Charge:** [REDELIVERY RULE]

---

## 7. Delays

The Business will make reasonable efforts to deliver orders according to the selected schedule.

Delivery may be delayed because of:

- heavy traffic;
- road closures;
- severe weather;
- accidents;
- public events;
- vehicle problems;
- unexpected operational issues;
- high order volume;
- third-party delivery issues;
- other circumstances outside reasonable control.

Where a significant delay occurs, the Business may contact the customer.

---

## 8. Business-Caused Delivery Problems

If the Business is responsible for a delivery problem, the Business will work with the customer to provide an appropriate solution.

Depending on the circumstances, this may include:

- completing the delivery;
- arranging another delivery;
- replacing an affected product;
- refunding an eligible amount;
- refunding an applicable delivery fee.

The applicable remedy may depend on the nature and seriousness of the problem.

---

## 9. Damaged Products During Delivery

If a cake or sweet product arrives damaged, customers should report the issue within:

**4 hours of delivery.**

Customers should provide reasonable information about the condition of the product and may be asked to provide photographs or other evidence.

Serious damage may qualify for:

- replacement;
- refund;
- another appropriate remedy.

Further details are provided in the **Cancellation & Refund Policy**.

---

## 10. Delivery Fee Refunds for Damaged Orders

Where the Business is responsible for damage occurring during delivery, an applicable delivery-fee refund may be considered.

The amount may depend on the delivery distance and the Business's delivery-fee structure.

**Delivery Fee Refund Rule:** [BUSINESS-SPECIFIC RULE]

A delivery-fee refund is separate from any refund or replacement that may be available for the damaged product itself.

---

## 11. Product Handling

Customers should handle delivered cakes and sweets appropriately after receiving them.

Where applicable, customers should follow any storage or handling instructions provided with the product.

The Business is not responsible for damage caused after successful delivery by improper handling, storage, temperature exposure, or other customer-controlled circumstances.

---

## 12. Special and Customized Orders

Customized cakes and other specially prepared products may require additional preparation time.

The Website may therefore provide specific delivery dates or time slots for such products.

Customers should ensure that the selected delivery schedule provides sufficient preparation time.

Once preparation has begun, cancellation of customized products may no longer be available according to the Cancellation & Refund Policy.

---

## 13. Delivery Restrictions

The Business may refuse or restrict delivery where:

- the destination is outside the supported delivery area;
- safe or reasonable delivery cannot be completed;
- the address cannot be verified;
- the location is inaccessible;
- delivery would violate applicable law or safety requirements;
- operational circumstances make delivery unreasonable.

Where an order cannot be fulfilled for a Business-controlled reason and a refund is applicable, the Business will handle the refund according to the Cancellation & Refund Policy.

---

## 14. Pickup

The Website may support customer pickup in addition to delivery.

**Pickup Available:** [YES / NO]

If pickup is enabled:

**Pickup Location:** [BUSINESS ADDRESS]

**Pickup Hours:** [PICKUP HOURS]

**Pickup Procedure:** [PICKUP PROCEDURE]

The Business may require the customer to provide order information or other reasonable verification when collecting an order.

---

## 15. Delivery Communication

The Business may contact customers using the contact information associated with their order for purposes including:

- delivery coordination;
- delivery status;
- recipient availability;
- address clarification;
- delays;
- failed delivery;
- other order-related issues.

Transactional SMS or OTP-based communication may be used where supported.

The Website is not currently intended to send promotional email communications.

---

## 16. Delivery and Privacy

Delivery requires the Business to process information necessary to fulfil the order.

This may include:

- customer identity/account information;
- contact information;
- delivery address;
- order details;
- requested delivery date and time;
- relevant delivery instructions.

Delivery information will be handled according to the Website's **Privacy Policy**.

Delivery personnel or service providers should receive only information reasonably necessary to complete the delivery.

---

## 17. Delivery Partner or Third-Party Courier

The Business may use its own staff or an external delivery service.

Where an external delivery provider is used, relevant order and delivery information may be shared with that provider to the extent necessary to complete the delivery.

The Business will select and configure delivery providers according to its operational and legal requirements.

**Current Delivery Provider:** [DELIVERY PROVIDER / IN-HOUSE]

---

## 18. Delivery Confirmation

The Business may record information confirming that an order was delivered.

This may include:

- delivery status;
- delivery timestamp;
- order identifier;
- relevant operational information.

Delivery confirmation may be used to manage orders, resolve disputes, and maintain order records.

---

## 19. Customer Responsibility After Delivery

Once an order has been successfully delivered, customers are responsible for appropriate handling and storage of the products.

This does not affect the customer's rights concerning products that were already damaged, incorrect, defective, or otherwise improperly supplied at the time of delivery.

---

## 20. Changes to Delivery Policy

The Business may update:

- delivery areas;
- delivery fees;
- delivery schedules;
- pickup availability;
- delivery partners;
- redelivery rules;
- other delivery procedures.

Changes will normally apply to future orders.

The **Last Updated** date and **Policy Version** will be updated when this policy changes.
