## 1. Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to personal information processed through the Website and related services operated by the Business.

It covers information collected when you:

- create or use an account;
- browse cakes, sweets, and other catalogue products;
- add products to a cart;
- place or manage an order;
- provide a delivery address;
- select product options or customizations;
- communicate with the Business;
- use personalization features;
- interact with the Website;
- contact the Business regarding privacy or customer-service matters.

This Privacy Policy does not replace the Website's Terms & Conditions, Cancellation & Refund Policy, Delivery Policy, or other applicable policies.

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect only information that is necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy and for providing the services you request.

### 2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we may process:

- email address;
- display name;
- username;
- password authentication information handled by our authentication service;
- profile biography, if you choose to provide one;
- website/profile information, if you choose to provide one;
- profile picture/avatar, if you choose to upload one;
- account role and account timestamps.

Passwords are handled by the authentication system and are not intentionally stored by the Website as ordinary readable password data.

### 2.2 Order and Delivery Information

When you place an order, we may collect information necessary to process and deliver that order, including:

- products ordered;
- quantities;
- selected weight or product options;
- customizations;
- order amount;
- order status;
- payment status;
- delivery address;
- requested delivery date and time;
- information necessary for customer support;
- order-related contact information.

Order records may contain a historical snapshot of the products and options included in an order so that the Business can maintain an accurate record of what was purchased.

### 2.3 Cart Information

If you use the shopping cart, we may store:

- the products added to your cart;
- quantities;
- selected product options;
- selected customizations;
- information associated with your account;
- cart timestamps.

### 2.4 Payment Information

The Website is designed so that Cake-Web does not intentionally store customers' payment credentials such as card numbers, banking credentials, or UPI authentication information.

Where an external payment service is used, payment processing may take place through that third-party provider.

The specific payment provider used by the Business will be identified or linked where required once the provider has been selected.

Available payment methods may include:

- UPI;
- net banking;
- cash on delivery;
- other payment methods introduced by the Business.

The Business may receive payment status or transaction references necessary to confirm and manage an order.

### 2.5 Communications

The Business may use contact information provided by you to communicate about:

- orders;
- delivery;
- account security;
- authentication or OTP verification;
- order status;
- customer-service matters;
- privacy or data requests.

The Website is not currently intended to send promotional email campaigns.

Transactional SMS or similar communications may be used where necessary for authentication or order-related communication.

### 2.6 Profile and Public Information

Some profile information may be publicly visible when you choose to maintain a public profile.

Depending on the Website's configuration, this may include:

- username;
- display name;
- biography;
- website/profile link;
- profile picture.

Private account information is not intended to be publicly displayed.

## 4. Personalization

The Website may provide a **Personalization** setting that allows a customer to control whether new activity information is collected or used for personalization purposes.

When Personalization is **ON**, the Website may, depending on the features implemented by the Business, use relevant information such as interactions with products, searches, purchases, classifications, or other permitted activity to provide personalized experiences.

When Personalization is **OFF**, the Website should stop collecting **new activity information for personalization purposes**.

Turning Personalization OFF does not prevent the Website from processing information that is necessary to:

- maintain your account;
- authenticate you;
- process orders;
- provide requested services;
- maintain order records;
- protect Website security;
- prevent abuse or fraud;
- operate necessary technical systems;
- comply with applicable legal requirements.

Personalization OFF therefore does not mean that all information processing stops.

Information previously collected for personalization purposes may remain stored unless it is deleted in accordance with an applicable deletion request, retention requirement, or other applicable policy.

Customers may request deletion of personalization-related information through the Business's privacy contact.

The Business intends to process such requests within **48 hours**, subject to verification and applicable legal or operational requirements.

The exact personalization features available on the Website may change over time and should be described in the Website's current Personalization & Data Controls Notice.
