# 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information that a Website may store or access through a user's browser.

Cookies and similar technologies can be used for purposes such as:

- maintaining secure login sessions;
- remembering necessary Website state;
- operating Website features;
- improving Website reliability;
- measuring Website usage;
- supporting security.

Not every Website feature requires cookies, and the Website may use other technical mechanisms for similar purposes.

# 6. Analytics and Personalization Are Different

Analytics and personalization are separate concepts.

### Analytics

Analytics is intended primarily to understand **overall Website usage**, such as how many people visit and which parts of the Website are being used.

### Personalization

Personalization concerns information associated with a customer or their interactions that may be used to provide a more individualized Website experience.

For example, a future personalization system may consider permitted signals such as:

- products viewed;
- searches;
- purchases;
- product classifications;
- repeated interactions;
- other permitted activity.

The Business may use analytics without using those analytics for individualized product recommendations.

---

# 7. Personalization Setting

The Website may provide customers with a **Personalization ON/OFF** setting.

The setting controls whether the Website may collect and use **new activity information for personalization purposes**, subject to applicable law and the configuration of the Website.

### Personalization ON

When Personalization is ON, the Website may collect and use permitted activity information for personalization features that have been implemented and disclosed.

Possible future features may include:

- personalized product relevance;
- recommendations;
- personalized catalogue experiences;
- other individualized experiences.

The Business will not treat the Personalization setting as permission to perform processing that is prohibited by applicable law.

### Personalization OFF

When Personalization is OFF, the Website should stop collecting **new activity information for personalization purposes**.

However, turning Personalization OFF does not stop information processing that is necessary for:

- account authentication;
- account security;
- placing or managing orders;
- maintaining order history;
- processing requested services;
- fraud prevention;
- security monitoring;
- technical operation;
- legal compliance;
- other necessary Website functions.

Therefore:

> **Personalization OFF does not mean that the Website stops processing all personal information.**

It means that new activity information should no longer be collected or used for the Website's personalization purposes.

# 13. Children's Data

The Website may be accessible to users under 18.

However, the Website must apply additional safeguards where required for children's personal data.

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, a child is an individual who has not completed 18 years of age, and the Act contains specific requirements concerning parental consent and restrictions on tracking or behavioural monitoring of children. citeturn0search17turn0search20

Accordingly:

- Personalization systems must not be designed to bypass children's privacy protections.
- Behavioural tracking of children must not be enabled where prohibited.
- Targeted advertising directed at children must not be introduced where prohibited.
- Any required parental-consent mechanism must be implemented before processing that requires such consent.
- Analytics and technical monitoring must be configured consistently with applicable requirements.

The Business must review its child-data configuration before enabling behavioural personalization in production.
